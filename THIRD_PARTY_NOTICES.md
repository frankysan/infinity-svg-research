# Third-party notices

This repository contains research tooling and metadata. It intentionally does **not** contain the
raw Infinity Army SVG corpus, Corvus Belli source PDFs, downloaded wiki/image archives, or generated
reconstructions derived from those assets.

Infinity, Infinity the Game, its graphical assets, logos, unit symbols, profile symbols, and related
artwork are owned by Corvus Belli and are not licensed under this repository's MIT license.

Research records may identify source assets by filename, URL, byte size, hash, and measured rendering
statistics. Those records are included for reproducibility and provenance and do not contain the
underlying artwork.

Third-party reference sources such as Human Sphere are treated as non-authoritative research inputs.
They must not silently replace current first-party artwork.
